## Samsung artwork

The following existing resources originate from the Samsung Fit3 plugin:

- `app/src/main/res/drawable-nodpi/fit3_preview_body.png`: band-body artwork.
- `app/src/main/res/drawable-nodpi/face_*.png`: bundled watch-face previews.

They are not original artwork by this project's author. Permission for public
redistribution has not been established in this preparation. Keeping them in
this local review copy is not a claim that they may be publicly redistributed.
Fit3 App grants no rights to use or redistribute this artwork. Its inclusion
does not place it under the project's GPLv3 license or establish permission
from Samsung. This notice does not itself authorize redistribution.

The official watch-face catalogue and additional previews are retrieved from
Samsung services at runtime. No Samsung APKs, firmware images, or downloaded
watch-face BIN packages are included in this source snapshot.
